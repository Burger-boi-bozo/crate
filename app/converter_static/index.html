A few useful things to know +

Preview before downloading. Crate checks the public page first and shows the title, creator, duration, and thumbnail when the source provides them.

Your server, your downloads. Downloads run in parallel, can be paused or resumed, and interrupted jobs are safely requeued after a restart.

Pick your quality. Choose the maximum source quality or a smaller video resolution. MP3 goes up to 320 kbps; original audio and video preserve the source codecs without re-encoding. Sources are never upscaled.

Music links. Spotify and Apple Music song links find public YouTube recordings for you to check and choose. They do not export subscription streams or guarantee an identical recording.

Source availability. Some sites require login or reject automated requests. Crate identifies those provider blocks separately from conversion failures.

Only download media you own or have permission to save. Follow the source site’s rules.
