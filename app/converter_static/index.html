A few useful things to know +

Keep it short. This small shared service handles one conversion at a time, with a daily allowance of 10 jobs. Long clips, playlists, live streams, and private or protected media aren’t supported.

A slow first visit is normal. Free hosting sleeps when idle. Give it about a minute to wake up.

Some sites may say no. YouTube and other sites can reject requests from cloud servers. If a clip fails, try another public link or a download provided by its creator.

Up to Full HD. Video uses the best available quality up to 1080p. Lower-resolution sources stay at their original size. Audio-only links need the MP3 option.

Music services have limits. Spotify and Apple Music subscription songs cannot be exported to full MP3 files here. Public Apple Podcasts episodes, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, and direct unprotected media links can be tried. We don’t substitute a different song or label a preview as a full track.

Only download media you own or have permission to save. Follow the source site’s rules.
